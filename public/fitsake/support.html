Help with Fitsake

Good outfits. Worth remembering.

Fitsake is provided by Eunoia Technologies LLC. For questions, feedback or privacy requests, email admin@eunoiatechnologies.io. You can also choose Send feedback in Fitsake Settings.

Tell us whether you use an iPhone or Android phone, your Fitsake version, and what happened. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details. Outfit photos and backups are not needed for a first support request.

Keep your outfits safe

Fitsake saves its own copies of imported photos. Your wardrobe lives on your device, with no automatic sync or app-managed cloud backup. Before deleting the app, clearing its data or moving to another phone, use Settings → Backup & restore to save a backup elsewhere. Keep the whole folder.

Restoring a backup replaces the destination library after you confirm. If a storage provider cannot open the backup, copy the complete folder to local Files or Documents first. Backup and restore are available on Free, even if the backup contains more than 10 outfits.

Fitsake Plus

Plus adds unlimited saved outfits. All other features are available on Free. When a subscription ends, you can still use your existing outfits, edit them, export them and restore backups. Adding another outfit while 10 or more are saved requires Plus. Monthly and annual subscriptions show your local price before purchase; the annual charge is paid in full each year.

Use Restore purchases with the same Apple or Google account used to subscribe. This restores eligible Plus access, not wardrobe photos. Apple and Google purchases do not automatically transfer between stores.

To cancel renewal, choose Manage subscription in Fitsake Settings or open your store's subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple or Google handles payment and refund requests.

Appearance

Settings → Appearance offers System, Light and Dark. Seasonal look is a separate choice. Both are saved only on this device. The launch screen follows the device appearance; your chosen app appearance applies when Fitsake opens.

Privacy and data requests

Your photos, notes, tags, pieces, plans and wear records stay in the local wardrobe unless you choose to export or share them. Billing providers and support email hold separate records. Uninstalling Fitsake does not delete exported or shared copies, store records or email.

Email admin@eunoiatechnologies.io for help with a privacy request. If it concerns a purchase, we may ask for its Fitsake order or transaction number to locate the record, then verify ownership before disclosing or deleting information. Do not send a full receipt unless requested; hide unrelated purchases and payment details. An order number alone is not proof of ownership.

Because Fitsake has no account, your email is not automatically linked to an anonymous billing record. We may be unable to identify a record without matching purchase information, including for Free users without a purchase. We will explain any limitations and information needed for your request. Subscription cancellation remains a separate action.

Read Fitsake's Privacy Policy for retention and request details, and Terms of Use for the product and subscription terms.
